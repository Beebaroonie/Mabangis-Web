Juego Todo
The Complete Game
What Is Juego Todo?
Juego Todo — Filipino for "total game" — is the philosophy of full combat engagement. It is not a single technique or style. It is the commitment to use every available tool: empty hands, bladed weapons, impact weapons, environment, and mind. When the game is total, no range is off-limits and no option is withheld.
The Principle
Most systems train you to fight within a comfort zone. Juego Todo destroys comfort zones. You train in every range — largo (long), medio (mid), and corto (close) — transitioning fluidly between them. A practitioner of Juego Todo is never out of their element because they have no single element.
Core Ranges
Largo Medio Corto Blade Impact Clinch Ground
Why It Matters
Real confrontations do not stay in one range. They change in fractions of a second. Juego Todo training prepares you to adapt, not react. The goal is not to win a match — it is to survive reality.
