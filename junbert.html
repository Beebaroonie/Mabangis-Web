The Ambassador
Junbert is the founding ambassador of The Lab — a practitioner who has trained, tested, and lived the principles of Juego Todo and Mabangis. The title Ambassador is not honorary. It is earned through years of consistent training, teaching, and representation of the art both inside and outside the gym.
The Mission
As ambassador, Junbert carries the art forward — not just preserving it, but evolving it. The Lab exists because of this commitment. Every class, every seminar, every posted piece of content is an act of stewardship. The goal is to ensure that what has been learned is not lost, and what is being built is accessible.
